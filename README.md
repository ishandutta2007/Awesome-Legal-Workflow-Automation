## Top Legal Workflow Automation Platforms Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on No-Code Legal Automation, Decision Trees, Intake-to-Resolution Workflows, Document Generation & Legal Ops*
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Legal Workflow Automation**. These systems help in-house legal teams and law firms digitize recurring processes—intake, triage, decision logic, approvals, document generation, and self-service tools—often with no-code or low-code builders.

**Examples** include BRYTER, Josef, Checkbox, Neota Logic, Onit Apptitude, LawVu, ServiceNow Legal, LegalFly, Mitratech TAP Workflow, and DoNotPay Business (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Enterprise legal workflow platforms are largely commercial. Practical open options include **Open Decision** (no-code automation designed for legal), experimental legal AI agent systems, and general open workflow engines that can be adapted. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the gap.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[BRYTER](https://bryter.com/)**  
  No-code platform for legal and compliance teams to turn complex, rule-heavy processes into digital workflows with audit trails and enterprise integrations.

- **[Josef](https://joseflegal.com/)**  
  No-code legal automation platform with visual flowchart builders, bots, forms, and document generation for self-service legal tools.

- **[Checkbox](https://www.checkbox.ai/)**  
  AI-powered legal front door and no-code workflow platform for intake, triage, contract/invoice review, and routing of legal requests.

- **[Neota Logic](https://www.neotalogic.com/)**  
  Expert system and no-code automation platform used to build intelligent legal applications and decision-driven workflows.

- **[Onit Apptitude](https://www.onit.com/)**  
  Workflow and intake automation capabilities within the broader Onit enterprise legal management suite.

- **[LawVu](https://lawvu.com/)**  
  Legal operations platform supporting matter management, contracts, spend, and workflow orchestration for corporate legal teams.

- **[ServiceNow Legal](https://www.servicenow.com/)**  
  Legal service management and workflow automation built on the ServiceNow platform for enterprise request handling and process automation.

- **[LegalFly](https://www.legalfly.com/)**  
  Legal AI and automation platform supporting workflow and document-centric legal processes.

- **[Mitratech TAP Workflow](https://mitratech.com/)**  
  Workflow automation and intake solutions within the Mitratech enterprise legal technology portfolio.

- **[DoNotPay Business](https://donotpay.com/)**  
  Consumer and business-oriented legal automation tools that streamline certain recurring legal and administrative processes.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[Open Decision](https://github.com/open-decision/open-decision)**  
  Free and open-source no-code platform designed for the legal market to automate workflows and responses to recurring requests. Supports visual process automation without coding.

- **[doc.haus and open-source legal AI agents](https://github.com/)**  
  Self-hosted multi-agent legal AI systems that support document review, playbook enforcement, tracked changes, and repeatable review workflows on infrastructure you control.

- **[GLAW and virtual law-firm agent systems](https://github.com/)**  
  Experimental open-source agent frameworks that model intake, matter handling, specialist routing, and multi-step legal workflows with hard-gated matters.

- **[General open workflow and BPM engines](https://github.com/)**  
  Open-source business process management and workflow engines (Camunda, Flowable, Temporal community editions, etc.) that legal teams can configure for intake and approval flows.

- **[Form and decision-tree open builders](https://github.com/)**  
  Open form platforms and decision-logic libraries useful for building guided legal intake and self-service questionnaires.

- **[Document generation and template open tools](https://github.com/)**  
  Open document assembly and template engines that support automated generation of contracts, letters, and legal forms.

- **[Rules and expert-system open frameworks](https://github.com/)**  
  Open rule engines and knowledge-based systems that can encode legal decision logic.

- **[Ticketing and service-desk open systems](https://github.com/)**  
  General open ITSM tools that can be adapted for legal request intake, assignment, and SLA tracking.

- **[Audit logging and evidence open helpers](https://github.com/)**  
  Components for maintaining immutable records of workflow steps and decisions—important for legal defensibility.

- **[Integration and connector open libraries](https://github.com/)**  
  Open connectors for linking workflow tools to CLM, matter management, email, Slack/Teams, and document systems.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Starting with **Open Decision** when a legal-specific no-code automation tool is desired and self-hosting is acceptable.
- Combining general open workflow engines with legal-specific forms and document templates for custom solutions.
- Experimenting with self-hosted legal AI agent systems for review and playbook enforcement (always with attorney oversight).
- Accepting that enterprise-grade no-code builders, deep integrations, AI-assisted triage at scale, and vendor-supported compliance features still favor commercial platforms (BRYTER, Checkbox, Josef, Neota Logic, Onit, LawVu, ServiceNow Legal, etc.).
- Focusing open-source efforts on internal process digitization and data ownership rather than replacing full legal operations suites.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Capture requests via open forms or chat → apply decision trees / rules in Open Decision or a BPM engine → generate documents from templates → route approvals → log every step for audit. Suitable for smaller legal teams or specialized internal processes. Most corporate legal departments continue to rely on commercial legal workflow platforms for scale, support, and enterprise integration.
